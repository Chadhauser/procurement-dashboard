# Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd — Capability Statement

## Who we are
Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd is a long-established family company (incorporated 1958)
delivering professional and business services to the public sector. We act as a
**prime contractor**, combining our own in-house finance and business-advisory
capability with a vetted supply chain, so buyers deal with a single accountable

## Our delivery model
We prime the contract and manage delivery through named, vetted subcontractors on
**back-to-back terms** — each carrying its own insurance and delivery obligations.
Our differentiator is financial: the business is led by a chartered management
accountant, so we bring genuine financial standing, disciplined contract and cost
management, and prompt payment through the supply chain.
