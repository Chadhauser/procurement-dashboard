# Edwards Bros — Bid Library

Reusable bid assets for Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd, bidding for public-sector
contracts as prime contractor and subcontracting delivery, with finance/advisory
delivered in-house through the associated practice Insight Professional Partners (IPP).

## How to use
Drop the relevant file(s) into a bid, or load them as knowledge into the bid workspace.
Every bid reuses most of these — so you never start from a blank page.

## IMPORTANT — read before using any of this in a live bid
1. **These are drafts.** Peter and Tammy must review, tailor and formally **adopt** each
   policy (add a named owner, a date and a signature) before it is submitted anywhere.
2. **Never fabricate past performance.** `past-performance.md` is a *template* to record
   contracts genuinely delivered. Where a tender demands comparable contracts you do not
   yet hold, name a committed subcontractor and rely on **their** track record (allowed
   under the Procurement Act 2023). Do not enter references for work that did not happen.
3. **Fill the placeholders.** Anything in `[ square brackets ]` is a real fact, figure,
   cert number, date or name that must be confirmed and filled before use.
4. **No overclaiming.** Do not claim a certification, accreditation or capability the
   business cannot evidence. Edwards Bros does not provide statutory external audit.
