# Insight Professional Partners Ltd (IPP) — Capability Statement

## Who we are
Insight Professional Partners Ltd (IPP) is the finance and accountancy practice that
delivers the financial and advisory workstreams behind Edwards Bros bids. IPP is a
CIMA-member practice (practising from February 2026), led by a chartered management
accountant.

## What we deliver
- Management accounting and financial reporting
- Financial modelling, analysis and business cases
- Budgeting, forecasting and cash/treasury management
- Financial systems, processes and controls improvement
- Final accounts and basic tax returns (Self Assessment and Corporation Tax)
- Internal audit for public bodies
- Finance-function and virtual finance-director support

## What we do not do
IPP does **not** provide statutory external audit. Where a requirement calls for
registered-auditor work, it is out of scope or delivered by a named specialist
subcontractor.
