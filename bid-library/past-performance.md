# Past Performance — template and guidance

## Read this first
Public bids score the bidder's **relevant, comparable contracts**. Only genuinely
delivered work goes here. **Never enter a reference for work that did not happen** —
fabricated past performance is fraud and ends the business.

## Where the company record is still building
Edwards Bros / IPP as bidding entities are building their public-sector contract
record. Two honest ways to satisfy a past-performance requirement in the meantime:
1. **Key-personnel experience** — lead with the genuine track record of the team
   (see key-personnel.md), where the tender allows individual experience.
2. **Subcontractor reliance** — where a tender demands X comparable contracts the
   company does not yet hold, name a **committed subcontractor** and rely on *their*
   past performance (permitted under the Procurement Act 2023). The sub must be named
   and their references used; do not present their work as our own.
