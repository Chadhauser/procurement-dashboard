## About your organisation

professional and business services to the public sector as a prime contractor,
combining in-house finance and advisory capability (through its practice arm IPP) with
a vetted, quality-assured supply chain. Led by a chartered management accountant, we
bring financial rigour, disciplined delivery and SME responsiveness.

## Approach & methodology
We start by confirming the requirement and success measures with the buyer, agree a
delivery plan with a named lead and milestones, deliver in reviewable stages, and
quality-check every key output against the requirement before issue. Where delivery is
subcontracted, we manage the sub to the same standard on back-to-back terms.

## Quality assurance
Each engagement has a defined scope, a named lead and independent second-person review
before issue; subcontracted work is specified clearly and acceptance-checked; client
feedback and lessons learned feed the next stage. (See Quality Assurance Policy.)

## Managing the contract and supply chain
As prime we are the single point of accountability. Subcontractors are vetted, hold
their own insurance, and work on back-to-back terms that flow down delivery
obligations. We pay our supply chain promptly and monitor performance against the
specification throughout.

## Business continuity
Our cloud-based, remote-capable operation continues through disruption; key-person risk
is mitigated by documented working papers, second-reviewer cover and our subcontractor
network. (See Business Continuity Policy.)

## Why choose us
Financial rigour and governance from chartered-accountant leadership; a single
accountable prime with a flexible, quality-assured supply chain; and the agility of an
SME with the discipline of a larger organisation.
