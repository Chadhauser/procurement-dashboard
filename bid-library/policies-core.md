## 1. Health & Safety Policy
Edwards Bros is committed to the health, safety and welfare of its employees,
subcontractors and anyone affected by its work, and complies with the Health and
Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and applicable regulations. We assess and control risks
for each engagement, ensure subcontractors hold their own competent H&S arrangements
and insurance, provide information and supervision, and record and review incidents.
As a predominantly office-/advisory-based business, our principal risks are display-
screen and lone/remote working, which are assessed and managed. Overall responsibility
rests with [named director]. [For site-based subcontracted work, the subcontractor's
H&S plan applies and is verified before work starts.]

## 2. Equality, Diversity & Inclusion Policy
We are committed to equality of opportunity and to treating everyone fairly,
irrespective of any protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010. We do not
tolerate discrimination, harassment or victimisation in recruitment, employment, or in
dealings with clients, suppliers and subcontractors. We select subcontractors on merit
and expect them to uphold equivalent standards. Concerns may be raised with [named
director] and will be handled confidentially.

## 3. Data Protection & GDPR Policy
Edwards Bros is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (registration
ZA900683) and complies with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We process
personal data lawfully, fairly and only for specified purposes; hold only what is
necessary; keep it accurate and secure; and retain it no longer than needed. We honour
data-subject rights, use written data-processing terms with subcontractors where
personal data is shared, and report notifiable breaches to the ICO within 72 hours.
Data protection lead: [named person].

## 4. Information & Cyber Security Policy
We protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information entrusted to
us. Edwards Bros holds **Cyber Essentials** certification (valid to August 2027). We
apply access control, strong authentication, patching, malware protection, encryption
of portable data, and secure disposal. Staff and subcontractors handle client data
only as authorised. Security incidents are reported to [named person] and managed and
reviewed. [Cyber Essentials Plus — pursue if a target contract requires it.]

## 5. Quality Assurance Policy
We deliver work that meets the agreed requirement, on time and to a professional
standard. Each engagement has a defined scope, named lead, and review before issue —
key deliverables are checked against the requirement and quality-reviewed independently
by a second team member. We manage subcontractors to the same standard through clear
specifications and acceptance checks, capture lessons learned, and act on client
feedback. Quality lead: [named person].

## 6. Environmental & Sustainability Policy
We minimise the environmental impact of our operations: reducing energy use and waste,
favouring digital-first and paperless working, minimising travel through remote
delivery, and considering environmental factors when selecting suppliers. We comply
with applicable environmental legislation. [A Carbon Reduction Plan to PPN-compliant
format will be prepared where a contract requires it.]

## 7. Social Value Policy
We recognise the social value public spending can create. As a local SME we support
the regional economy, use local subcontractors and SMEs in our supply chain where
practical, pay our supply chain promptly, and offer skills and experience opportunities
where a contract allows. We tailor a specific social-value offer to each tender's
priorities. [Add measurable commitments per bid.]

## 8. Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy
Edwards Bros prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms and complies with the
Bribery Act 2010. We do not offer, give, request or accept bribes or improper
inducements, and we require the same of our subcontractors. Gifts and hospitality must
be reasonable, proportionate and recorded. Concerns may be reported to [named director]
without fear of reprisal.

## 9. Modern Slavery & Human Trafficking Statement
Edwards Bros has a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and human trafficking
(Modern Slavery Act 2015). We expect our subcontractors and suppliers to operate
ethically and lawfully, and we take steps proportionate to our size to ensure slavery
and trafficking are not taking place in our business or supply chain.

## 10. Business Continuity Policy
We maintain the ability to continue delivering during disruption. Our advisory work is
cloud-based with backed-up data and remote-working capability, so operations can
continue from any location. Key-person risk is mitigated by documented working papers
and a second reviewer on each engagement, and by our subcontractor network for
delivery capacity. Continuity arrangements are reviewed [annually]. Continuity lead:
[named person].
